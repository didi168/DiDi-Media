About Me

Web & Mobile Developer | Creative Designer

I'm a skilled and innovative Web & Mobile Developer and Creative Designer with 7+ years of combined experience spanning video editing, motion graphics, and full-stack development. With expertise in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js, Next.js, TypeScript, and Node.js, combined with cross-platform mobile development and professional skills in graphic design, video editing, and motion graphics, I bring a unique blend of technical and creative expertise to every project.

My experience spans developing responsive web applications, building intuitive mobile interfaces, creating brand identities, and producing high-impact marketing materials. My flagship project, Workalat, is a full-stack freelance marketplace platform where I serve as both Frontend Developer and Video Editor/Motion Designer — building the user-facing application while producing the marketing content that drives its growth. I've worked with various brands to deliver engaging visual content and robust technical solutions, ensuring that every project I touch is both functional and aesthetically pleasing.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Environmental Biology from the University of Port Harcourt and have volunteered as both a Computer Science Teacher and Graphics & Video Editing Instructor, sharing my passion for technology and creativity with aspiring learners.
